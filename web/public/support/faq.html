Questions, fixes and glossary

Questions

Why can I only have three active priorities?

On purpose. Exe is built to help you finish a few things, not to track everything at once. Everything else waits in Next or Later, and nothing is lost.

Why does “Status” say “Nothing here yet”?

A new Exe starts empty and nothing is added for you. Capture a thought with the blue + button, or make a folder and an outcome in the Map.

I ticked every step, but the outcome is not done.

That is by design. Only you finish an outcome. Press Mark outcome done…, or answer the question Exe asks when the last step is ticked.

I ticked a task in the List, but my step is not done.

An List tick only marks that task. Steps, decisions and priorities are separate on purpose.

What does “Blocked” mean, and why is it red?

Something you confirmed must be finished first. Red is used only for that. A suggested relationship blocks nothing.

Can the AI change my work?

No. It can only make suggestions on cards, which you approve. It cannot delete, complete, resolve, or move priorities.

Does AI cost money?

Exe does not charge for AI. If you add your own key, your AI company bills you for what you use.

How do I undo something?

Approvals in AI Review have an Undo button under Recently approved. Archived items have Restore. A finished outcome has Reopen outcome. A resolved decision has Reopen decision.

Can I delete something for good?

Exe prefers archiving. Deleting a folder keeps everything inside. Deleting a link keeps both items.

Fixes

If you see…Try this
Nothing happens when I save textRefresh the page and try again. If it keeps happening, download a backup, then tell whoever runs your Exe.
“Saved on this device, not synced”You are offline or the service is unreachable. Keep working; it sends itself when the connection returns.
“This changed on another device”See Your account, saving and privacy.
Another device’s changes are missingRefresh the page. Exe does not update live.
Cannot sign in or the confirmation email never arrivedCheck your spam folder. Then try Create an account again with the same email, or ask whoever runs your Exe.
“AI is not set up on the server yet”The site owner has not finished AI setup. Everything else works.
“Your provider rejected the key” / “does not know that model name”Open Settings, check the key and the model name exactly as your provider shows them, and save again.
The page looks old or out of dateClose the tab and open it again, or hard refresh (Ctrl/⌘+Shift+R).

Shortcuts

Ctrl/⌘+KQuick Capture, from anywhere.
/Search (when you are not typing in a box).
EscClose the open box, or leave Focus Mode.
TabMove between buttons and fields. Everything in Exe can be used with a keyboard.
EnterIn Add a task…, adds it to your List.

Glossary

Capture
A thought you saved, exactly as you wrote it. It is never removed by Exe or AI.
Outcome
A result you want. Outcomes are what you make active.
Step
One small action that moves an outcome forward. It can also be loose.
Folder
An optional group for related outcomes, decisions, ideas and captures.
Decision
An unresolved question you settle yourself.
Idea
A possibility, not a commitment.
Relationship
A link between two things: blocks, informs or related.
Active priority
An outcome you are working on now. At most three. Also called “Now”.
Next / Later
Outcomes waiting their turn. Next is sooner, Later is parked.
Current Priority
The one active outcome shown in the big card on Focus.
Suggestion
Something Exe or AI proposes. It does nothing until you approve it.
