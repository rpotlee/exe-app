Turning on AI with your own key

Exe works fully without AI. For suggestions and drafts, you connect your own account with an AI company and pay them directly. Exe has no AI bill and never sends anything on its own.

What AI can and cannot do

  • Can: suggest priorities and groups, notice what depends on what, and prepare drafts, checklists and answers for a task.
  • Cannot: delete, complete, send, or change your priorities. Everything is a suggestion you accept.
  • Every answer is checked against a strict format. If the AI answers badly, Exe ignores it.

Set it up

  1. Sign in (AI needs an account, because your key is stored encrypted on the server).
  2. Get an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini or Groq, and note the model name your provider gives you.
  3. Open Settings → AI (your own key). Choose the provider, type the model name, paste the key, and press Save key.
  4. Read the notice, then press I understand, turn on AI.
  5. Press Test connection. It sends only a tiny test message, no personal text.
Careful. Your key is shown only as its last 4 characters after you save it. Treat it like a password. If you lose track of it, press Remove key and make a new one with your provider.

Which provider?

OpenAI and Anthropic need prepaid credit. Groq has a free plan with usage limits (try the model openai/gpt-oss-20b). Gemini’s free tier is not available in every region or account. Free plans may keep or use your prompts, so check the provider’s privacy terms before sending anything personal.

What is sent

Only when you ask (or open the assistant on a task), Exe sends that task’s text, or for suggestions the text of your open tasks and your folder names, to your provider. Nothing is sent otherwise.

Turning it off

Turn off stops all AI. Remove key deletes the stored key. Replace key saves a new one.

Good to know. If you see “AI is not set up on the server yet”, the site owner has not finished setup. Everything else still works.
