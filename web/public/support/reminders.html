Reminders

A reminder shows a banner at the top of Exe when its time comes.

Set one

  1. Press the type button in the List and choose Reminder (or choose Reminder in the capture box).
  2. Write what to be reminded about. Pick Remind me at, or press a quick time such as Tomorrow morning.
  3. Press the arrow. Reminders need a time, so the button stays off until you pick one.

A task can have a reminder too: open its three dots and use Remind me.

When it is due

A banner appears with 1 hour and Tomorrow to snooze it, and Dismiss to clear it. Dismissing only clears the reminder; the entry stays in your List.

Careful. Reminders show only while Exe is open. Exe does not send notifications, texts, emails or calendar invitations. If you have the page open or come back to it, a due reminder appears within about 30 seconds.
