The AI assistant for a task

Open a task’s details to restore its saved help. Opening Exe, importing entries, browsing and opening task details do not generate AI work. To ask for new help, press Prepare help or type a question. It needs AI turned on (AI setup).

A list review runs only when you press Review next. It analyzes up to four entries once, suggests priorities and relationships, and stops. It does not prepare drafts for every entry. Drafts and answers are prepared for the individual task you ask about.

If your provider reaches a limit, Exe pauses AI and shows a countdown. Your entries and typed question are kept. When the countdown ends, you choose whether to try again; Exe does not restart a background sweep.

What it does

  • Asks one question at a time when it needs to (for example whether a message should be a text or an email).
  • Offers at most three suggested actions, with the rest under More.
  • Produces drafts, checklists or short answers, and keeps earlier versions when you refine one (“Shorter”, “More formal”).
  • Remembers the conversation for that task.

What it never does

  • It never sends anything. Copy puts a draft on your clipboard, and you send it yourself.
  • It does not book, call, buy, look things up live or complete anything for you.
  • Links it gives are searches you open yourself.
Careful. AI can be wrong. Check facts, names, dates and amounts before you use a draft.

Each thing it prepares is logged in the Activity Log.
