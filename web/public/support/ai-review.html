AI Review

AI Review is where new captures get sorted. Exe suggests; you decide. Nothing is organized until you press an approve button, and reviewing is always optional.

Good to know. Until you turn on AI with your own key (see how), the suggestions come from simple word-matching rules, not an AI model. The screen says so. With AI on, a Get AI suggestions button appears.

What you see on each card

  • Your original capture, exactly as written.
  • Suggested type: Task, Outcome, Decision, Idea, or Just a note. Change it if the guess is wrong.
  • Suggested folder. Exe only suggests a folder whose name appears in your text.
  • A short reason, and (with AI on) what the AI was unsure about.

What you can do

ButtonWhat it does
Approve & organizeCreates the item (a step, outcome, decision or idea). Your capture is kept and marked organized. A step with no matching outcome asks where it goes (see below).
Not nowMoves it to Set aside. Nothing is lost, and there is no timer. Review again brings it back.
RejectDoes not apply the suggestion and never changes your capture. Exe remembers, so the same suggestion is not offered again unless you change the type or folder.
Split…Turns one capture into several items. Edit the pieces, pick a type for each, press Split. The original stays whole.
Preview mergeAppears when two waiting captures look alike. You see both and the result. Merge makes one item; both captures are kept and linked to it.
Approve selected (n)Tick the box on several cards, then press this. A preview lists exactly what will be created before you confirm.
Approve allApproves every waiting capture with its current suggestion. Anything you rejected before is skipped, and steps with no matching outcome stay loose.

Where does this step go?

If a task has no matching outcome, Exe never invents one. It asks: leave it loose (no outcome), add it to an existing outcome, or create a new outcome for it. Loose steps are listed in Map → All notes, and you can attach them later with the pencil button.

Undo

Recently approved lists your last approvals with an Undo button. Undo removes only what the approval created, and only while you have not worked on it (for example, a step you ticked, or an outcome that now has steps, cannot be undone). The original capture always returns to the review list. Undone approvals appear in History.

Tip. With many captures waiting, the list shows 30 at a time. Use Show 30 more.
