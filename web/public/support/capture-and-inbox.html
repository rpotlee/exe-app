Capture and Inbox

Capture first, organize later. The Inbox is a normal checklist that works with no AI at all.

Quick Capture

  • Tap the Capture something bar above the tab bar (always visible on a phone) or Ctrl/⌘+K on a computer. It works from every screen, including inside Focus Mode, and returns you to where you were.
  • Several thoughts at once: put each on its own line. If there are two or more lines and each is short (up to about 100 characters), or any line starts with -, *, • or a number like 1., each line becomes its own to-do and the list markers are removed.
  • One long thought: a paragraph, or lines that are long, stays as one item with its line breaks.
  • Empty text cannot be saved. If you close the box, your unsaved text is kept as a draft while this browser tab stays open.

The Inbox

  • Add a to-do at the top adds one quickly without the capture box. Press Enter to add it and keep typing. Paste a whole list and each line becomes its own to-do. Use Shift+Enter for a line break inside one to-do.
  • All / Active / Done filter the list, each with a count. Newest items are first. In Done, the most recently finished is first. Very long lists show 200 at a time with Show 200 more.
  • Tick the circle to mark a to-do done. This is only a checkmark on that to-do. It does not complete any step, decision or priority.
  • The ⋯ button on a row opens its details: when and how it was captured, whether it awaits review, and its folder.

Row actions

  • File in: put it in a folder (or leave it Not filed).
  • Edit: change the text. Your original wording is always kept and shown as Original capture.
  • Archive hides it without deleting. View archived at the bottom shows archived items, where Restore brings one back.

Help while you build your list

If you have turned on AI (see Turning on AI), Exe looks at new to-dos a few seconds after you stop typing and quietly adds suggestions. Every one is labeled AI, none of it changes your list by itself, and you can ignore all of it. Without AI, the list simply works as a checklist. If AI is off, a small Turn on AI help link appears above the list.

  • Things that go together. A card named for the group lists the related to-dos and, when it spots one, a possible order (what to do first). Group these makes an outcome in Later with those to-dos as its steps. The suggested order is saved as an unconfirmed link: it does not hold anything up until you confirm it in Map. Not now hides the card. Your original to-dos stay in the Inbox.
  • Email drafts. When a to-do is about writing to someone, an Email draft appears under it. Open it, read it, fill in any [brackets], then Copy it or choose Open in email. Exe never sends anything.
  • Where to look. For research to-dos, such as how to register a business, Exe names a few official sources. Each link opens a search on that organization's own website, so you land on real pages rather than an address the AI guessed.
  • Quick answers. For simple facts, such as the capital of Australia, a short answer appears labeled AI answer with a link to check a source. Anything uncertain, recent, legal, medical or financial is sent to Where to look instead of answered. AI can be wrong, so check the source for anything important.
  • If AI cannot be reached, a short AI help paused message appears with Try again. Your list is never affected.

What is sent: only the text of your active to-dos, to the AI company you chose, using your own key. See Account and privacy.

Good to know. Exe never deletes a capture for you, and AI can never delete one.
