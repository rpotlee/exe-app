Your account, saving and privacy

Is my work private?

  • Yes. Each account has its own private workspace. The database enforces that every row belongs to one owner, so other accounts cannot read yours.
  • Exe does not sell or share your data and does not send anything to an AI company unless you turn AI on and press a button (see AI setup).
  • Your AI key is stored encrypted on the server and is never sent back to your browser.

How saving works

The status at the top right (computer) tells you where things stand:

SavedYour latest changes are stored in your account.
Saving…A change is being sent.
Saved on this device, not syncedYou are offline. Your changes are kept on this device and sent automatically, in order, when the connection returns. Retries never create duplicates.
Needs your attentionAnother device changed the same thing. See below.

Using more than one device

Sign in with the same account on each. Open Exe and your latest saved work is there. Exe does not update live while it is open, so refresh the page to see changes made on another device.

When two devices change the same thing

Exe never silently overwrites. It shows This changed on another device, and nothing was overwritten. Your unsent changes are set aside and newer changes wait. Press Download my unsent changes to keep a copy, then Use the latest version. Redo anything you still want.

Your data and the free plan

Keep a backup. Some hosting plans have no automatic backups, and a service that is not used for a while may pause until its owner resumes it. A backup file keeps your work safe either way.
