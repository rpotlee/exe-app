How can we help?

Exe helps you get things out of your head, decide what matters, and start the next useful step. It never changes your work behind your back.

Getting startedYour first 10 minutes, step by step. Focus and your prioritiesThe home screen, up to three active priorities, Focus Mode. Questions and fixesPlain answers, and what to do when something looks wrong.

The five ideas behind Exe

  1. Capture anything, instantly, without sorting it. Use the blue + button.
  2. Review it when you are ready. Exe can suggest what each thought is. You approve or reject.
  3. Map your work into folders, outcomes (results you want) and small steps.
  4. Decide open questions in one place, and see what each one is holding up.
  5. Focus on at most three things at a time, and start with one clear step.
