How can we help?

Exe is a simple list that quietly helps. Write things down, tick them off, and, if you choose, let AI suggest priorities, groups and drafts. Nothing changes without you.

Getting startedYour first 10 minutes, step by step. The ListTasks, notes and reminders, with no AI needed. Status and All tasksWhat to work on, grouped. Questions and fixesPlain answers, and what to do when something looks wrong.

The five tabs

  • Status: your top tasks and your groups.
  • List: everything you save. Quick, checkable, always works.
  • Activity (Activity Log on a computer): a timeline of what the AI did and what you decided.
