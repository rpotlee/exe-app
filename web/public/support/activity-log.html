Activity Log

Open Activity (called Activity Log on a computer) to see, newest first and grouped by day, what the AI suggested or prepared and what you chose to do with it.

  • AI lines are things it did: priorities suggested, a group suggested, a draft prepared, a failure.
  • You lines are choices you made from its suggestions, for example accepting a group or changing a priority.

The log stores small facts only, never your full text or drafts. It is for review; nothing here can be changed.
