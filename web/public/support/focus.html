Focus, priorities and Focus Mode

Focus is your home screen, called Mission Status. It shows what you are working on now, and quietly offers help choosing what to do next. Nothing on it changes unless you press a button.

The status line

Under the heading you see something like 1 current focus of up to 3 active. 2 for the AI Assistant. 3 blocked. Parts that are zero are left out. Awaiting AI Review is a link. Blocked counts only blocks you confirmed.

Current Priority

  • The large card is your current outcome: its folder, progress (real steps done, never invented), a checklist, and Enter Focus Mode.
  • On a phone the card shows your first three steps; press More to see the rest.
  • If something you confirmed is holding it up, a red Blocked notice names the blocker and gives a link such as Open the decision or Review in Map.

Other active priorities

Up to two more active outcomes appear below. Tap one to make it the current one.

Active, Next and Later

Every outcome sits in one of three places: active (at most three), Next, or Later. The Next & Later row at the bottom of Focus opens a screen listing the outcomes that are not active, with Make active and Move to Next / Later. If you already have three active, Exe asks which to replace, and does it as one change. Nothing moves by itself.

Priority suggestions

Exe can suggest changing which outcomes are active. It follows fixed rules, in this order, and shows the rule that decided: 1) you pinned it, 2) it is not blocked by a confirmed blocker, 3) the earliest deadline you entered, 4) it unlocks the most confirmed work, 5) it has a ready step, 6) you worked on it most recently. There is no secret score.

  • Apply… shows before and after and asks you to confirm. Ignore makes it go away until the facts change. Defer hides it for now.
  • A suggestion can also point out an open active slot, with a reason.

I have … minutes

Choose 10, 20, 30 or 60. Exe suggests one ready step that fits: not blocked, and not known to take longer. It prefers your active priorities and explains why. It uses the effort you typed on steps (like 15 min). A step with no estimate may be suggested but is labeled “may not fit”. It never guesses an estimate, never looks at your calendar, and never changes your priorities. Start in Focus Mode takes you to that outcome.

Prompts after changes

Up to three notes may appear: a resolved decision that was holding things up, a finished step that cleared the way for another, or an outcome whose steps are all done (asking whether it is finished). Review takes you there; Later hides it and it does not come back. They never change anything.

Weekly review (optional)

Once a week a card says your review is ready. Open shows the last 7 days from real dates: steps done per outcome, outcomes finished, decisions resolved, thoughts captured, and what is still open. There are no streaks or scores. Skip this week or Done hides it until next week.

Break it down

For a vague step, press Break it down (in Focus Mode, or on an outcome in the Map). Type or paste one small step per line; list dashes and numbers are removed and duplicates ignored. Nothing is created until you press Add steps. If AI is on, Suggest steps with AI fills the box with ideas you can edit or delete first.

Focus Mode

Press Enter Focus Mode on the Current Priority card. You see only that outcome and its checklist.

  • Tick steps as you finish. Progress is real.
  • Capture thought opens Quick Capture over Focus Mode and brings you back afterwards. New thoughts go to the Inbox, not into your active work.
  • Break it down adds steps. Return to Exe or Finish session (or Esc) leaves. If every step is done, Exe asks whether the outcome is finished.
