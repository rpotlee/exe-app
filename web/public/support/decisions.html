Decisions

A decision is an unresolved question, like “Which city should we launch in?”. Keeping decisions apart from tasks shows you which unanswered questions are slowing you down.

The list

  • Open decisions come first, ranked by how much confirmed, unfinished work they are holding up, then by the earliest deadline you entered on that work, then newest. Each row says holding up N. Resolved decisions are listed last.
  • It is never ranked by how convincing the text sounds.

Working on a decision

  • New decision writes a question (optionally with context and a folder). Edit question changes it later.
  • Add option adds a possible answer. Click an option to edit it, or remove it.
  • Evidence is for facts and sources you checked. Keep it separate from guesses. Research and notes is free space for thinking.
  • What matters to you (criteria): write the things you care about (budget, time, risk).
  • Comparing options: open an option and write What is good about it and What is not so good. Once two or more options have notes, a Side by side table appears. Exe lays out your own words and never scores or ranks options for you.

Resolving

  1. Press Mark resolved.
  2. Pick the Chosen option (or write something else), write Your decision, and optionally Why.
  3. Press Confirm decision.
  • Resolving records your choice and the date. It never completes tasks, removes blockers or moves priorities. If the decision was holding things up, Exe shows Suggested follow-up and a prompt on Focus. You review those yourself.
  • You can Reopen decision any time. The previous conclusion stays visible.
  • The Decision log shows each change with its date.
Tip. To say a decision is holding something up, add a blocks relationship in the Map from the decision to the step or outcome, and confirm it.
