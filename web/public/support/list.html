The List: tasks, notes and reminders

The List is where everything you save lives. It never needs AI, and it is always fast.

Three types

TaskSomething to do. It has a round checkbox. Pasting a list makes one task per line.
NoteA thought or some info. It is never split up and has no checkbox.
ReminderSomething to be reminded of at a time you choose. See Reminders.

Add something

  1. Type in the box at the top. The small button on its left shows the type (Task, Note or Reminder); press it to change.
  2. Press the arrow (or Enter for a task).

Change or organize an entry

Press the three dots on a row. You can change its Type, move it to a Folder, set a Priority (tasks), set Remind me, Edit it, or Archive it. Your original words are always kept.

Priority

Priority is shown as a small colored dot: red is High, orange is Medium, yellow is Low. A filled dot is one you chose. A ring is the AI’s suggestion. Your choice always wins. In Settings, Priority labels lets you show AI suggestions, only your own, or none.

Finding things

Use the filter at the top (All, Tasks, Notes, Reminders). Finished tasks move to Done at the bottom of the list; tap it to open. View archived shows what you archived, and Restore brings it back. Very long lists show 200 at a time (Show 200 more).

AI help on entries

Exe checks entries on your device and can show a small label such as Can draft this, Can answer this, Research available, Can help break this down or Preparation available. This check does not contact your AI provider. Tap a label to choose the help you want, or open an entry’s three dots and press Ask Exe. Only that action sends the entry to your provider. Drafts are never sent to other people, and suggested searches are not verified sources.
