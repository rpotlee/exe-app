Settings, backup and restore

Open Settings from the left menu (computer) or by tapping your initial at the top right (phone).

Workspace name

Type a name and press Save name. It appears in the menu.

Download a backup

Press Download JSON backup. You get a file containing your folders, tasks, notes, reminders and everything else you saved. Please do this now and then. It is your safety copy.

Restore or import

  1. In Settings, under Restore or import, choose a backup file. It can be one downloaded from this version, or the file exported by the original Exe prototype.
  2. Exe shows what is inside (counts of each kind) and any warnings. Nothing has changed yet.
  3. Pick how to add it: Add to what I have keeps everything already here (recommended), or Replace everything with this backup.
  4. For Replace, press Download a copy of my current workspace first, so you can go back. Then press the final button.
  • Original wording is kept, and nothing is marked done that was not.
  • Relationships that could not be kept (a missing item, a loop or a duplicate) are skipped and counted in the warnings.
  • Files that are not backups, or are too large, are refused without changing anything.

History

Open history lists, in plain words, what you approved, applied or undid. For what the AI did, see the Activity Log.

Priority labels

Choose whether to show the AI’s suggested priorities, only the ones you set, or none.

AI

See Turning on AI with your own key.

Signing out

Open Settings and press Sign out (on a computer it is also in the left menu). Closing the browser tab does not sign you out. If you are using Exe on a shared computer, sign out when you finish.
