Ideas and Search

Ideas

An idea is a possibility, not a commitment. It does not appear in your priorities and is never turned into work unless you say so. Ideas live in the Map under Ideas worth keeping.

  • Add idea: write it, add notes, choose a folder. Approving a capture as an Idea in AI Review also creates one.
  • Edit changes it. Archive hides it, and Show archived ideas then Restore brings it back.
  • Promote asks you to confirm, then creates an outcome in Later (never active). The idea is kept, archived, and linked to the outcome.

Search

  • On a phone tap the magnifier at the top. On a computer use Search in the left menu, or press / when you are not typing in a box.
  • It searches captures (archived ones are labeled), steps, outcomes, decisions (including evidence and notes), ideas and folders. Type one or more words; every word must match.
  • It is plain text matching. It never guesses what you meant. Results that start with your words come first.
  • Pick a result to jump to it.
