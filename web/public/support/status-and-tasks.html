Status, All tasks and groups

Status is your home screen.

All tasks

The large card shows your top tasks (3 on a phone, 5 on a computer), high priority first. Press View all tasks to open the full screen: tasks sorted into High, Medium, Low and No priority, with a Done section you can open.

The AI mark

A small sparkle on a task means the AI has looked at it. Filled means it has a question for you. Press it to open details and the assistant. Tapping the task text just shows its details.

Groups

Under the card, Groups collects related tasks. Each group shows how many tasks it has and how many are High, Medium and Low. Tasks that are not in a group wait in Not grouped yet. Open a group to see its tasks and the AI help for each.

The AI can suggest a group name for tasks that belong together. It only becomes a group when you accept it.

Good to know. Everything on these screens is real. If you have no tasks, Status says so instead of showing examples.
