Groups and All tasks

Groups brings related List entries together.

Progress and timing

The overall ring counts each visible task or to-do once. Each group card shows its own completed percentage and remaining tasks. Missed includes unfinished work with a passed deadline or active reminder; Upcoming covers the next seven days. Tap a tile to see its items.

The AI mark

A small sparkle on a task means the AI has looked at it. Filled means it has a question for you. Press it to open details and the assistant. Tapping the task text just shows its details.

Groups

Each card shows its name, completion percentage, and remaining entries. Ungrouped is always present. Open a group to see its tasks and any suggested dependencies. Use New group to create a group and add tasks yourself.

The AI can suggest a group name for tasks that belong together. It only becomes a group when you accept it.

Good to know. AI suggestions never replace groups you created or corrected.
