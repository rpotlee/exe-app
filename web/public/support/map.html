Map: folders, outcomes, steps and dependencies

The Map answers “how does my work fit together?”. You can use Exe without ever opening it, but it is where you build the structure that Focus uses.

The pieces

FolderA group, like Home or Work. Optional.
OutcomeA result you want, for example “Confirm business registration”. Outcomes are what you make active.
StepOne small action that moves an outcome forward. A step can also be loose (no outcome).
RelationshipA link between two things, such as “this must finish before that”.

Folders

  • All notes (top of the list) shows everything, including items not yet filed. On a phone, tap the highlighted folder to open the list.
  • New folder creates one. Rename changes its name.
  • Delete folder never deletes what is inside. If it holds items, Exe asks where they should go: another folder, or All notes (not filed).

Outcomes

  • Add outcome: name it, and (optionally) write the Definition of done.
  • Edit outcome: change the name, definition of done, folder, deadline and effort. Deadline and effort are your own entries. Exe never makes up a date.
  • Pin as priority tells the priority suggestions to keep it ahead. Unpin removes that.
  • Make active, Move to Next and Move to Later choose where it sits. At most three can be active. See Next & Later.
  • Mark outcome done… asks you to confirm using your own definition of done. Finishing every step only asks; it never marks the outcome done by itself. Completing frees an active slot, and nothing is moved in automatically. Reopen outcome puts it back in Later.
  • An outcome with no steps shows Not measured, never 0%.

Steps

  • Add step: one action. Break it down: type or paste several, one per line (see Break it down).
  • Tick a step when it is done. This records the step and when you last worked on the outcome.
  • The pencil opens Edit step: rename it; move it to another outcome (or make it loose); give it an Effort estimate such as 20 min or about 2 hours; a Deadline; and Notes. Move up / Move down reorder; Archive step hides it from progress (a Restore button appears under Archived steps).

Relationships and dependencies

Press Add relationship, choose From, the kind, and To.

KindMeaning
blocksThe “from” item must be finished first. Only a confirmed block holds anything up.
informsUseful context. It never holds anything up.
relatedConnected, with no order.
  • Tick Save as a suggestion only if you are not sure. It is labeled Suggestion and blocks nothing until you press Confirm. Remove deletes only the link, never the items.
  • Exe refuses a link to itself and any loop (A blocks B blocks A), and tells you why.
  • Items held up by a confirmed block show a red Blocked label. Red is used only for confirmed blocks.

Also in a folder

Ideas (see Ideas) and captures you filed there are listed at the bottom.
