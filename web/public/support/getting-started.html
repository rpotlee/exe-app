Getting started

Follow these steps once and you will know how everything fits together. Each step links to a longer page if you want more.

On this page
  1. Sign in
  2. Capture your first thoughts
  3. Look at your List
  4. Make a folder and an outcome
  5. Add steps and choose what is active
  6. See your focus and start
  7. Optional: AI
  8. Back up your work

1. Sign in

  1. Open Exe in your browser. On a phone you can add it to your home screen: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; in Chrome open the menu, then Add to Home screen.
  2. Choose Create an account, enter your email and a password of at least 8 characters, then press Create account.
  3. If you are asked to confirm your email, open the message we sent and follow the link. Then return and press Sign in.
  4. You land on Status. It says Nothing here yet. That is correct: a new Exe is empty, and nothing is added for you.
Good to know. Your workspace is private to you. If Exe was set up to run on this device only, there is no sign-in and your data stays in this browser.

2. Capture your first thoughts

Capturing is the one thing Exe wants to be effortless. You do not have to say what a thought is, where it belongs, or when it is due.

  1. Press the blue + button (on a computer you can also press Ctrl+K, or ⌘+K on a Mac).
  2. Type or paste anything on your mind. To add several at once, put each one on its own line:
    Call the plumber
Book flights
Renew passport
  3. Press Save. Each line becomes its own task in your List. A long paragraph stays as one item.
Tip. If you close the box before saving, your text is kept as a draft and is there when you reopen it (as long as this browser tab stays open).

3. Look at your List

Open List in the bottom bar (phone) or the left menu (computer). It is a plain checklist: newest first, with All, Active and Done filters. Tick a box to mark a task done. That changes nothing else. See Capture and List.

4. Make a folder and an outcome

An outcome is a result you want, like “Book the venue”. A folder groups related things, like Home or Work.

  1. Open Map, then press New folder. Name it and press Create folder.
  2. Press Add outcome. Write the result you want. In Definition of done, say how you will know it is finished (optional but helpful). Press Add outcome.
  3. The new outcome starts in Later. You choose what becomes active.

5. Add steps and choose what is active

  1. On your outcome press Add step and write one small action. Or press Break it down and type several steps, one per line.
  2. Press Make active to make this outcome one of your active priorities. You can have at most three. If three are already active, Exe asks which one to replace.
Good to know. The limit of three is on purpose. Exe is built to help you finish a few things, not to hold everything at once.

6. See your focus and start

Go to Focus. The big card is your Current Priority: its progress, its next step, and a button Enter Focus Mode. Focus Mode hides everything else so you can work on one outcome. Tick steps as you finish them. When every step is done, Exe asks whether the outcome is finished. That is always your decision. See Focus, priorities and Focus Mode.

7. Optional: AI

Open AI Review to look at your new thoughts. Without AI, Exe makes simple word-based guesses and tells you so. You can approve, reject or put a suggestion aside with Not now. To get real AI suggestions, you add your own key in Settings. See Turning on AI. Nothing is sent anywhere until you turn it on and ask.

8. Back up your work

Open Settings and press Download JSON backup. Save the file somewhere safe. Do this now and then. See Settings, backup and restore.

You are set

That is the whole loop: capture, review, map, decide, focus. Everything else in Exe is a way to make one of those steps easier. Questions? See Questions and fixes.
