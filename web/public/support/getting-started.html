Getting started

Follow these steps once and you will know how everything fits together.

1. Sign in

  1. Open Exe in your browser. On a phone you can add it to your home screen: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Choose Create an account, enter your email and a password of at least 8 characters, then press Create account.
  3. If you are asked to confirm your email, open the message we sent and follow the link. Then return and press Sign in.
Good to know. Your workspace is private to you.

2. Save your first thoughts

  1. Press the blue + button (on a computer you can also press Ctrl+K, or ⌘+K on a Mac).
  2. Type or paste anything on your mind. To add several tasks at once, put each on its own line:
    Call the plumber
Book flights
Renew passport
  3. Press Save. Each line becomes its own task in your List.

3. Use your List

Open List. Tick the round box to finish a task. Use the three dots on a row to change its type, move it to a folder, set a priority, edit or archive it. Details are in The List.

4. See what to work on

Open Groups to see related tasks and their progress. Use New group to create one yourself. See Groups and All tasks.

5. Optional: turn on AI

Exe works fully without AI. If you want suggestions and drafts, follow Turning on AI with your own key.
