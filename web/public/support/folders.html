Folders

Folders are separate workspaces. All shows everything together.

  1. Press the folder name at the top (computer) or open the folders control on your phone.
  2. Choose All or a folder, or press New folder, name it, and press Create folder.

While a folder is chosen, the List, Groups and Activity show only that folder, and new entries go into it. In All, new entries are unfiled. You can move an entry from its three dots. Delete folder keeps everything inside it (it becomes unfiled).
